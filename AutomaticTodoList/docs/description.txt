[line]

[heading]Installation / 安装[/heading]
[list]
[*]Install the latest version of [url=https://smapi.io/]SMAPI[/url].
安装最新版本的 [url=https://smapi.io/]SMAPI[/url]。
[*]Download this mod from Nexus Mods and unzip it into Stardew Valley/Mods.
从 Nexus Mods 下载本 Mod，解压到 Stardew Valley/Mods 文件夹。
[*]Run the game using SMAPI.
使用 SMAPI 启动游戏。
[/list]

[line]

[heading]How to Use / 使用方法[/heading]
[list]
[*]By default, the to-do list appears in the top left corner of your screen.
默认情况下，待办清单显示在屏幕左上角。
[*]The default hotkey to show/hide the list is LeftShift + L (configurable).
默认的显示/隐藏快捷键是 LeftShift + L（可配置）。
[*]Tasks appear and are checked off automatically based on in-game conditions.
任务会根据游戏内的不同情况自动出现并自动勾选。
[/list]

[b]Example / 示例：[/b]
On the morning of a villager's birthday, a task like "Give XYZ a birthday gift" will appear. Gifting that villager on that day checks it off.
某位村民生日当天的早晨，清单上会出现 "给 XYZ 送生日礼物" 的任务。当天给该村民送礼即可自动勾选该任务。

[line]

[heading]Supported Tasks / 支持的任务类型[/heading]
Each task type can be toggled on/off individually in the settings.
以下任务类型均可单独在设置中开启或关闭（每种均可独立开关）。

[list]
[*][b]Villager birthdays[/b] – reminds you to gift the birthday villager.
[b]村民生日[/b] – 提醒给过生日的村民送礼。
[*][b]Harvestable crops[/b] – shows ready-to-harvest crops (flowers can be included/excluded via config).
[b]可收获的作物[/b] – 显示已成熟的作物（可通过配置包含/排除花朵）。
[*][b]Unwatered crops[/b] – alerts you to crops needing water.
[b]未浇水的作物[/b] – 提醒需要浇水的作物。
[*][b]Ready machines[/b] – indicates machines that have finished processing (including machines inside buildings).
[b]已就绪的机器[/b] – 显示已完成加工的机器（包括建筑内部的机器）。
[*][b]Harvestable crab pots[/b] – shows crab pots with ready catch.
[b]待收获的蟹笼[/b] – 显示已有收获的蟹笼。
[*][b]Tools ready for pickup[/b] – notifies when upgraded tools are ready at Clint's.
[b]待取回的工具[/b] – 克林特处升级完成的工具。
[*][b]Daily quest board[/b] – reminds you to check the board outside Pierre's.
[b]每日任务板[/b] – 提醒检查皮埃尔商店外的任务板。
[*][b]Special Orders Boards[/b] – reminds you to check for special orders.
[b]特别订单板[/b] – 提醒检查特别订单板。
[*][b]Traveling Merchant cart[/b] – reminds you to visit the cart when it's present.
[b]旅行商人[/b] – 当旅行商人出现时提醒拜访。
[*][b]Queen of Sauce[/b] – alerts you when a new recipe is available on TV.
[b]酱料女王[/b] – 当电视播出新食谱时提醒收看。
[*][b]Gifting specified villagers[/b] – lets you set custom villagers to gift regularly.
[b]指定村民送礼[/b] – 可自定义需要定期送礼的村民。
[*][b]Coop & Barn animals[/b] – reminds you to pet your farm animals (chickens, cows, etc.).
[b]鸡舍和畜棚动物[/b] – 提醒抚摸农场动物（鸡、牛等）。
[*][b]Pet[/b] – reminds you to pet your dog or cat.
[b]宠物[/b] – 提醒抚摸你的狗或猫。
[/list]

[line]

[heading]Configuration / 配置[/heading]
All settings are fully compatible with Generic Mod Config Menu (GMCM).
所有设置均与 通用 Mod 配置菜单（Generic Mod Config Menu） 完全兼容。
You can toggle each task type, adjust the hotkey, and customize the panel appearance.
您可以在 GMCM 中开关各类任务、调整快捷键以及自定义面板外观。

[b]New Visual Options (recent update) / 新增视觉选项（最近更新）[/b]
[list]
[*][b]Show Panel Background[/b] – hide the background box and draw only text.
[b]显示面板背景[/b] – 隐藏背景框，只显示文字。
[*][b]Panel Shadow[/b] – toggle a shadow behind the panel background.
[b]面板阴影[/b] – 切换面板背景后的阴影。
[*][b]White Text[/b] – draw panel text in white instead of dark (for better visibility).
[b]白色文字[/b] – 使用白色文字代替深色文字（提高可见性）。
[*][b]Text Shadow[/b] – toggle a shadow behind each text line.
[b]文字阴影[/b] – 切换每行文字后的阴影。
[*][b]Text Underline[/b] – draw an underline beneath each list item.
[b]文字下划线[/b] – 为每个列表项添加下划线。
[/list]

[b]Harvestable Crops Options / 可收获作物选项[/b]
[list]
[*][b]Include Flowers[/b] – when enabled, harvestable flowers (tulips, sunflowers, etc.) are included in the crops check. Default: off.
[b]包含花朵[/b] – 开启后，可收获的花朵（郁金香、向日葵等）将纳入作物检查。默认：关闭。
[/list]

[b]Panel Improvements / 面板改进[/b]
[list]
[*]The panel is now scrollable with a scrollbar (use mouse wheel or drag, similar to GMCM).
面板现在可滚动，并带有滚动条（可使用鼠标滚轮或拖拽，类似 GMCM 风格）。
[*]The panel title automatically follows the current game language (i18n support).
面板标题会根据当前游戏语言自动切换（i18n 支持）。
[*]Mouse wheel scrolling only works when the cursor is over the panel.
鼠标滚轮滚动仅在光标悬停于面板上时生效。
[*]Scrollbar opacity matches panel opacity; a plain style without background is available.
滚动条不透明度跟随面板不透明度；支持无背景的简约样式。
[*]Overflow indicator (scroll hint) is retained.
保留溢出指示器（滚动提示）。
[/list]
